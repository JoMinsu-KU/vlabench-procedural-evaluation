# Provisional claim matrix

Status: **PROVISIONAL — do not cite as final**

This matrix records the claim boundary before the source-to-registry audit and
leave-one-task/category-out analyses. It must be reviewed and frozen again
after those checks. Existing result labels remain the authoritative baseline;
if the registry audit identifies a substantive mismatch, affected labels are
reviewed without overwriting their original provenance.

| Topic | Supported provisional claim | Prohibited interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| A–B relationship | The two interfaces expose complementary planning-responsibility levels and produce interface-conditioned classifications for the same model–case pairs. | Experiment B is a universally superior, stricter, or interchangeable replacement for Experiment A. |
| Primary finding | Independently elicited symbolic and reference-procedure outputs show substantial model–case-level discordance and low success-set overlap. | An Experiment-A plan was transformed into or degraded in Experiment B. |
| Responsibility boundary | Experiment B reallocates discrete phase, order, and step-specific target decisions from the benchmark executor to model-visible fields; geometry and continuous control remain backend-owned. | The VLM performs low-level, end-to-end, pose, trajectory, joint, or gripper control. |
| Reference procedure | The registry operationalizes one fixed benchmark-derived reference-procedure contract followed by native task evaluation. | The registry enumerates every physically valid plan or proves that nonconforming plans are physically invalid. |
| Scaffold conditions | Separately elicited reference-phase and reference-target scaffolds are associated with aggregate outcome changes relative to Full B. | Same-plan repair, causal decomposition, monotonic oracle improvement, or an exact ranking of causal bottlenecks. |
| Symbolic normalization | Raw and type-normalized symbolic endpoints demonstrate representation sensitivity; rate ordering is not treated as metric superiority. | The sign of A-minus-B establishes which interface is better. |
| Failure taxonomy | Observed runtime termination and responsibility-adjudicated attribution are reported separately. | First-termination counts estimate all latent errors in a plan. |
| Corrected gate | Offline corrected-gate revalidation supports the adjudicated target/context mapping without changing original success labels. | The original runtime produced no C5 exceptions or the corrected gate was used in the original runs. |
| Bootstrap intervals | Task-cluster bootstrap intervals assess sensitivity to the empirical composition and weighting of the 34 retained tasks. | Inference to a superpopulation of unseen VLABench tasks. |
| Manifest scope | Findings are conditional on the fixed 2,133-case dual-replay-qualified non-complex analysis manifest. | Results characterize the complete 4,000-case non-complex distribution or all of VLABench. |
| Model scope | Findings apply to the three evaluated open VLM backbones under the frozen prompts and serving conditions. | Universal claims about VLMs, model scale, or prompt-optimal performance. |
